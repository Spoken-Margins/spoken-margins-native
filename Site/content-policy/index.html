Spoken Margins

Content policy for share links

Version 1.0 (draft) · Effective date to be set when counsel has reviewed it

A share link lets you send a Margin to a friend. This page says what may not be shared that way, how to report a link, and what happens next.

Draft. This page has not been reviewed by an attorney yet. Share links are not open to everyone yet; this page describes how reports will be handled when they are. It is not legal advice, and it summarizes the Terms of Use without replacing them.

What may not be shared

  • Intimate images of someone without their consent. See Removing intimate images.
  • Child sexual abuse material. It is never allowed.
  • Harassment, threats, or sharing someone’s private information.
  • Hateful conduct: content that attacks people for who they are, such as their race, religion, ethnicity, disability, sex, sexual orientation or gender identity.
  • Content you have no right to share. For copyright, see Copyright and DMCA.
  • Malware and spam.

A share link is built to carry your own comment with a short quotation of the source around it, not the full source text. To send a whole item, send the .spokenmargins file directly (AirDrop, Messages or Files). That never passes through our server.

How to report a link

Anyone can report, with no account. Open the link and use Report this link at the bottom of its page. Or email cole@proximitytoprogress.com with the link and your reason. Leave off everything after the # sign, which is the key; the part before it is enough to find the link. For child sexual abuse material, use the form instead: it is the only route that lets us forward the full link.

  1. Choose what the problem is.
  2. If you reported another link in the last 7 days, add a short note on why. One word is enough. The link is taken down either way.
  3. You get a reference number.

What happens next

  • The link comes down at once. A report filed through the form disables the link the moment it is filed, in every category. A report sent by email is acted on by a person, within the time below.
  • Nobody reads the content. Spoken Margins can’t read what a link holds, and we don’t open it. We act on the report, not on a judgment about the content.
  • The sender sees one notice: “A link you shared was taken down after a report”. It is the same for every category and doesn’t say who reported or why. We don’t contact the sender about the report.
  • Strikes. Two reported links from the same installation within 90 days make a strike. Strike 1 pauses link sharing for 1 week. Strike 2 pauses it for 1 month. Strike 3 ends it for good on that device. This applies to every category, copyright included. Listening, opening links and sending .spokenmargins files directly always keep working.
  • A person answers your report within 3 business days. Intimate-image requests are handled within 48 hours.
  • Child sexual abuse material. We forward these reports unread to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children’s CyberTipline, and keep the reported link in restricted storage for one year, as federal law asks. Nobody opens it.

Reporters

We want reports. If you report often and tell us why, you are welcome. We block a device from reporting only if the reasons it writes show that it is misusing the button, and only after a person decides. Not answering our message is never a reason. If the button confuses people, we change the button.
