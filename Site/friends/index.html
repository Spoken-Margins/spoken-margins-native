Spoken Margins

A friend sent you Margins

Short answers to the questions people ask when a friend sends them Margins.

What did my friend send?

Margins: short voice or written notes your friend made while listening to an episode or reading an article. Each one is pinned to the moment it's about, so you hear it at that point in your own copy.

Do I need the app?

No. A link plays in any web browser, on a phone or a computer. A .spokenmargins file opens at app.spokenmargins.com with Open a Margin file. On iPhone or iPad, Spoken Margins opens both. The app is in beta through TestFlight: see Try Spoken Margins.

The link or file won't open. What now?

A link works for 30 days and has to be copied whole. If it has expired or been cut off, ask your friend to share it again. If you're offline, reconnect and try again. If our server had a problem, try again in a minute. If links are switched off, ask your friend to send the Margins as a file. If a file is too big, ask for fewer Margins at a time. If it needs a newer Spoken Margins, update the app. If the Margins don't match your copy, check you have the same edition of the episode or document.

Where did my friend's Margins go?

In a browser, they're on the page the link opened and under Margins, kept in that browser. In the app, open the Margins tab and tap Shared, or find the episode or article in your Library: until you've heard them, its screen has a button with your friend's name that plays their Margins.

Why can't I hear the episode?

Your friend sent their Margins, not the episode. It's fetched from where it's published, and sometimes it can't be: it was taken down, it's in a private or paid feed, or a browser can't play that audio. Your friend's Margins still play without it. A browser can't read an article aloud yet: open the article, or open the link in Spoken Margins on iPhone.

Why does it ask me to add the episode or article?

Margins are pinned to moments in your own copy, and the file didn't bring one. Add the same episode, article or document to your Library and your friend's Margins land in their places. They wait on that screen until you add it or cancel. For a private podcast feed, paste your own feed link and Spoken Margins finds the episode.

Why is a Margin a little off?

Your copy of an episode can differ from your friend's, usually because the ads are different. When it does, Spoken Margins says how far off the Margins may be, and a Margin it couldn't place plays after the others. In an article, a Margin it couldn't place says so, and you can tap it to listen anyway.

How do I answer?

Record or write a Margin while theirs plays, or within 20 seconds after it ends, and it's saved as a reply. In the app, you can also open their Margin in the Margins tab and tap Reply, then tap Share to send your replies back. In a browser, tap Save as a file (or Send, where your browser offers it), then send the file to your friend in a message.

Who hears my answer?

Only the people you send it to. Nothing leaves your phone or browser until you share it, and you pick which of your Margins go. A link someone sends you is locked with a key that's only in the link: our server can't read it, but anyone who has the link can.

How do I get rid of them?

In the app, open About this file on your friend's card and turn their Margins off, or tap Hide this Margin or Hide this sender. Nothing is deleted, and Settings can show them again. In a browser, they're kept only in that browser: clearing the site's data removes them. To report a link, open it and tap Report this link.

Still stuck? Ask the friend who sent it, or write to us from the Support page.
