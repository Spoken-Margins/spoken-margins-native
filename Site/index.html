A margin you can hear

Listen to a source, interject in your own voice, and export the resulting thread into the notes or knowledge system you already use. Spoken Margins keeps raw captures private and makes sharing an intentional, sender-reviewed action.

Direct sharing

A friend can send you a .spokenmargins file through Messages, Signal, Mail, AirDrop, or Files. The package contains reviewed interjections and anchors—not the podcast, article, full source text, or source audio.
