Removing intimate images

Version 1.0 (draft) · Effective date to be set when counsel has reviewed it

If someone shared an intimate image of you through a Spoken Margins link without your consent, you can ask us to take it down. This includes images made or altered by a computer. You don’t need an account.

Draft. This page has not been reviewed by an attorney yet. Share links are not open to everyone yet; this page describes how reports will be handled when they are. It is not legal advice, and it summarizes the Terms of Use without replacing them.

How to ask

  1. Open the link and use Report this link at the bottom of its page, and choose “Intimate images shared without consent”.
  2. Or email cole@proximitytoprogress.com with the details below.

The link stops working the moment you file a report through the form. If you have reported another link in the last 7 days, the form also asks for a short note on why; one word is enough, and the link still comes down at once. We don’t need to see the image, and nobody at Spoken Margins looks at what a link holds.

What your request needs

  1. The link, or enough to find it. Leave off everything after the # sign: that part is the key, and we don’t need it. The part before it tells us which link to remove.
  2. A statement that you believe in good faith that the image shows you, or the person you act for, in an intimate way, and that it was shared without consent.
  3. Your physical or electronic signature, or that of the person who may act for you.
  4. A way to reach you. Your contact details are used only to answer your request.

What we do

  • We act within 48 hours of a valid request, and usually much sooner. A report filed through the form disables the link at once.
  • We send you a reference number.
  • Spoken Margins can’t read what a link holds, so we remove the link we are given. If you find other links to the same image, send those too.
  • We don’t tell the person who made the link who reported it or why. Their app shows only “A link you shared was taken down after a report”. The notice is the same for every kind of report.
  • Repeat reports against the same sender can end that person’s ability to create share links.

If the image shows a child

Don’t email it to us. If you are or were under 18 in the image, or know of child sexual abuse material, use the form’s child-safety category, or report at report.cybertip.org. Reports made through our form are forwarded unread to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children, and we keep the link in restricted storage for one year, as federal law requires. A report you file directly at the CyberTipline does not reach us.

Other help

You can also report to the platform that carries the link, to the police, or to StopNCII.org. Our content policy, which covers other kinds of reports, is on its own page.
