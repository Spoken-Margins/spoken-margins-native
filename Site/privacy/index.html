Spoken Margins

Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026 - Beta version 0.1

Spoken Margins is a local-first reading and listening app. Your library, recordings, transcripts, listening history, and settings stay on your device. The app connects to outside services to retrieve sources you select or follow, to fetch introductory content from our server, and when you deliberately share or export content.

What stays on your device

Imported text and files, saved links, listening maps, playback history, Margin audio and transcripts, session exports, and settings are stored locally. Spoken Margins has no account or sign-in system and includes no advertising, analytics, or tracking service.

Introductory content from our server

The app checks for the introductory tour from spokenmargins.proximitytoprogress.com when it opens, unless you have deleted that tour. The starter podcast catalog is fetched when you open it, including when the catalog opens on first use. These requests do not send your library, recordings, transcripts, listening history, or an installation identifier. Like other web requests, they expose a network address to the server while it responds.

Our web server is configured without request access logs. Its error logging removes client network addresses and request headers. We do not use these requests for analytics or tracking.

Source and podcast requests

When you import or play a link, Spoken Margins contacts the selected website, podcast feed, or media host to retrieve the requested material. This can include episode pages, publisher chapter and transcript files, artwork, and episode audio. Apple-hosted artwork in the starter catalog is fetched from Apple's image servers. To resolve podcast links, the app may send the selected link to Spotify's public metadata service or Apple's public podcast search and lookup services. For a Spotify link, the episode title can also be sent to Apple's search service. Source hosts and these services receive information normally included in a network request, such as the requested URL and network address, and handle it under their own terms and privacy policies.

Followed shows and websites are checked for new items automatically when the app opens or returns to the foreground. Article import can also look for a feed to follow, including on Substack. A private feed address may contain a token; that address is sent to the feed's host. Articles are loaded in a private, hidden web view, with outside resources blocked and cookies discarded afterward.

Podcast playback and offline downloads use media URLs supplied by publishers. Episodes can download automatically on Wi-Fi; cellular downloads require your action. A publisher transcript can be fetched automatically after import. The app may also retrieve episode audio to process the context of a recorded Margin on the device.

When you confirm an import of Margins someone shared with you, your device can fetch the source address in the packet. That source's host sees the request. Spoken Margins does not send your recordings, transcripts, library, or internal database identifiers to the developer.

Read Later services

If you connect Readwise Reader or Raindrop.io, Spoken Margins sends the access token you enter to that service when you connect or request more saved items. The token is stored in your device's Keychain. The app does not change your account at the service. Articles you choose to import can then be retrieved from their original websites.

On-device speech and intelligence

Spoken Margin transcription requests on-device speech recognition. iOS may first download a speech model from Apple. Installed system voices, bundled voices, and supported local intelligence features process their input on the device. Availability depends on the operating system, installed resources, language, and device capabilities. Spoken Margins does not operate a server for this processing. Remote OpenAI features are unavailable in this build.

Sharing, extensions, and support

The Share and Safari extensions write the content you explicitly share or capture into the app's private shared container. The Safari extension reads Selected Text or Readable Page when you invoke that action; it does not continuously read browsing activity.

When you use the system share sheet, copy an export, or email support, the destination you choose receives the content you select. A shared packet can contain the Margin recordings and text, source references, and other material shown in its review. Recipients can keep or forward their copy. This build shares packets as files; it does not upload them to a developer-hosted sharing service. Diagnostics are created locally for you to review and share, and are not sent automatically.

Deletion and retention

You can move library items to Recently Deleted, permanently delete them in the app, or remove the app to remove its local app data. Deleting your local copy does not remove copies you have shared with other people or services. An upgrade from an earlier private beta may archive an old local test database before creating the current database; the app reports that archive in its diagnostics.
