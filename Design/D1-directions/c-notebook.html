What you get, what it costs

  • Says what the app is for. A new listener sees Margins first, so “talk back to what you hear” is obvious. Sharing, the weekly note and the book club all live on the home screen, which is why this has the lowest tap counts.
  • Recording from anywhere. The Margin button is always one tap away and always means the same thing.
  • Cost: on day one the Notebook is empty, so it leans on first run and the “one to try” card. Following and Library share a tab, which is fine at 60 shows but may crowd a heavy Library. The transcript player needs a transcript or text; podcasts without one fall back to the chapter list.

Brief checklist

First run · Home (the Notebook) · Following (Listen › New; “60 shows”) · Library (Listen › Library) · Margins (the Notebook, with Mine / Friends / Search) · Now Playing with recording and Skim · Share · Settings · reviewing friends’ new Margins (Tuesday Readers). All content is made up.
