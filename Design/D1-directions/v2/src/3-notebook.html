3 · Notebook

What you said is the home screen. Listening is the other tab. The mic sits between them and never moves.

What you see first

Notebook: this week’s Margins, one line each, with a share icon. One row for the week (Export) and one for friends, only when they’ve said something new. Two tabs, Notebook and Listen, with the mic in the middle. The mini-player sits above them while something plays.

Disclosure tiers

On screenYour Margins with share, Export, friends’ new Margins, hold to record, the mini-player.
One tapListen (Continue, New, Saved), the player, Settings, the club’s review.
Two tapsAll 23 new, all Saved, manage 60 shows, add a link, diagnostics, first run again.
Never on landingFeed management, browsing, settings, anything about listening that isn’t a Margin.

Theory of mind

  • First minute. Follow one show, Start listening. The Notebook is empty on day one, so first run says what fills it and the Listen tab is where you land.
  • Most days. Listen tab: Continue is the top card. Say something with the mic; the Notebook fills.
  • The sharing moment. Every Margin on the home screen has a share icon. Tap it, tap the person.
  • Sunday. Export is the first row of the home screen.

Where it strains
