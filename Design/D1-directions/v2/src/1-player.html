The app is the thing you’re listening to. Everything else waits behind two doors.

What you see first

Now Playing, full screen: the words being read, your Margins beside them, and one big mic. Two doors in the top corners: Shelf (what to listen to) and Notebook (what you’ve said). Nothing else.

Disclosure tiers

Three tapsDiagnostics, first run again.
Never on landingFeed management, export, settings, badges.

Theory of mind

  • First minute. Follow one show, tap Start. You’re listening. The mic is the only bright thing on screen, so you try it.
  • Most days. Open the app, it’s already where you left off. Shelf when you want something else.
  • The sharing moment. You just said something. The Margin appears in the text with a share icon. Tap it, tap the person.
  • Sunday. Notebook shows the week with one Export button.

Where it strains

  • Browsing 60 shows happens two taps deep, in a list. Fine for the listener who follows a few; thin for someone who browses daily.
  • With nothing playing, the home screen needs a “Start” state. In this sketch it shows the last item.
  • Export is the only task at the ceiling. It’s weekly, so that was the trade.
