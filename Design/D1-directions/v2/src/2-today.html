Open it like a paper. What’s new from your shows is the front page; what you saved and what you said are the other two sections.

What you see first

Today: one Continue card, then what’s new from your shows. Your last Margin sits at the bottom with a share icon, and the book club appears there only when friends have said something new. Three tabs: Today, Saved, Notes. The mini-player has the mic.

Disclosure tiers

On screenContinue, four new items, share your last Margin, the mini-player’s play and mic.
One tapAll 23 new, Saved, Notes (with Export), Settings, the player, friends’ Margins.
Two tapsManage 60 shows, add a link or file, diagnostics, first run again.
Never on landingFeed management (#141), settings, empty sections.

Theory of mind

  • First minute. Follow one show, Open Today. The front page has one thing to play, and the mic is on the mini-player once it plays.
  • Most days. Open the app, see what’s new, tap one. Continue is always the top card.
  • The sharing moment. Your last Margin is on Today with a share icon. Older ones live in Notes with the same icon.
  • Sunday. Notes › Export saves the week to your notes folder in one tap. The first time, it shows the note and asks for the folder.

Where it strains

  • Today is only as good as the shows you follow. With none, it needs the first-run list back on the page.
  • Export saves without a preview after the first time. If you want to see the note every week, that’s a third tap.
  • Three tabs plus a mini-player is the most chrome of the three directions.
