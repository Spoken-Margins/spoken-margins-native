B · The Deck

The player is the app. You open Spoken Margins to what you’re listening to, with a big hold-to-talk button under your thumb. Home, Following, Library and Margins are panes of one drawer that slides up from the bottom and goes away when you pick something.

Try a task

To record, press and hold the yellow button, then let go. A quick tap only shows a hint.

Where things live

Root The Deck (Now Playing): follow, Margins on this item (⇧), next Margin, Skim, hold to talk, write Top Settings · Tuesday Readers · Add Drawer Home | Following | Library | Margins (peek bar opens it at the pane you tap) Sheets Manage 60 shows, Share, Export, Write

What you get, what it costs

  • Talking back is the fastest thing in the app. One press, no screen change, no double-tap hazard (#148). Suits listening while walking or driving.
  • Fewest places. One screen plus one drawer. Nothing to learn about tabs.
  • Cost: browsing is always a sheet over the player, which feels cramped for 60 shows or a big Library. When nothing is playing, the deck needs a good empty state (the first-run sample covers day one). Hold-to-talk needs teaching and is harder with VoiceOver (a Write button and a Settings switch to tap-to-talk cover that).

Brief checklist

First run (task “Follow a podcast on first run”) · Home, Following (Manage = 60 shows), Library, Margins in the drawer · Now Playing is the root, with recording and Skim · Share · Settings (gear) · reviewing friends’ new Margins (Tuesday Readers pill). All content is made up.
