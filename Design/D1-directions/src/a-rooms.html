What you get, what it costs

  • Familiar. Closest to Build 29 and to Apple Podcasts. Testers already know where tabs are, so it’s the cheapest for D2–D6 to build.
  • Home fixes onboarding. The “follow one show” card and the club card live where people land.
  • Cost: four places to look is still a lot for a new listener, and recording is one level down (open the player first, or use the mic on the mini-player).
