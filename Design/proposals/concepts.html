What each costs, in taps

Counted the way the brief counts: a menu item is two, the file picker's own taps are in the job. Blue is at or under the two-tap target. Amber is over the three-tap ceiling.

The pick: Four tabs

The numbers barely separate the three structures. Most of the saving comes from path fixes they all share: Margins newest first, a row that plays its moment, Share this Margin, Export all as one button, Settings one tap away. What separates them is whether a newcomer can guess where things live.

  1. Four tabs names four of six newcomer questions with a word on the tab bar. A show is a place you go, so the three-times-a-day show is one row with a count and can never flood anything.
  2. Stream is the right one if you listen by day rather than by show. Watch for this: if on Four tabs you open three shows in a row to collect the day's episodes, switch to Stream.
  3. Up next is your own idea, and it hands the app a judgment call. It wins only if your first tap after launch is Up next, not Library.
  4. Same tabs is the control. It gets everything but the two Manage feeds jobs under the ceiling and changes nothing a current listener would notice.

Every phone follows the brief's fixed rules: two taps target, three ceiling; feed management never on a landing screen; blue and orange for status with a label beside the colour; targets of 44 points; nothing new where an existing tap could carry the job.
