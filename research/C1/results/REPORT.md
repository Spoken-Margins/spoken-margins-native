## Cost model

Assumptions, stated so they can be argued with: narration at 155 words per minute, so every clutter word costs 0.39 s and every missing body word the same. A reading-order break costs 10 s (the listener loses the thread), a mid-sentence interruption 4 s on top of its words, a citation read as a bare number 1.5 s, a number glued to a word 2.0 s, an unrepaired hyphenation 1 s.
