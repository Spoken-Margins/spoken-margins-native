Which reading sounds right?

Both clips are the same fallback voice (KittenTTS). They differ only in how the text is turned into speech sounds before the voice reads it. One is the current open-source component we can't ship; the other is our replacement. Every clip is matched for loudness.

  1. Use headphones and play X and Y for each passage.
  2. Pick X better, Same or Y better. Judge pronunciation and how natural it sounds, not the words chosen.
  3. Tap Save choices. The answer key is a separate file; don't open it until you've saved.
