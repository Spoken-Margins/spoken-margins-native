# M4 surface map

Preserve native semantic text styles, `ViewThatFits`/stacked controls, accessibility labels, and existing recovery actions; do not replace them with the prototype's local success state.

## Existing layout traps to avoid

- `ExperienceAddSourceFlow`, `ExperienceSourcesScreen`, `ExperienceItemDetailScreen`, and `ExperienceTimelineRailScreen` currently use prominent editorial headers. For routine populated states, they should be compacted before adding another card or new explanation.
- `ExperienceItemDetailScreen` already has the correct primary actions before most supporting sections; do not bury them beneath a taller cover/metadata hero.
- `ExperienceAudioPreparationQueueScreen`, `ExperienceStorageScreen`, and sender sharing have necessary explanatory copy. Move it beside the destructive or consentful action or behind a disclosure; do not delete the warning.
- `SettingsScreen` opens with a dense AI section. Reordering user-facing playback/library controls above advanced provider/model detail is presentation-only and must not weaken consent, provider, or diagnostics controls.

## Scope boundary

The current native code supplies the underlying M4 actions above. M2's session-qualified star/explicit play and M3's written capture remain prerequisite runtime work; M4 views must not invent substitute row state, source matching, readiness, storage, permission, or sharing success while those interfaces are absent. No builds, tests, simulator interaction, or human validation were performed for this map.
