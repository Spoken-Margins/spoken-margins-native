Two follow-ups remain:

It does not execute `AppleSourceTranscriptionClient.transcribeAcquiredSourceAudioWindow`, so it would still pass if the production long-form or chunk adapter omitted or doubled the offset. Add a focused adapter-level test/seam with a nonzero `sourceStartMs` and local timestamps, asserting the resulting absolute times exactly once.

Once that acquisition is nonnil, `Gate1AppModel` does not take its original-URL fallback. No current production conformer hits this combination: the Apple adapter overrides both methods. Make the defaults a compatible pair or ensure this specific unsupported-adapter error takes the bounded fallback; add a legacy-conformer regression.
