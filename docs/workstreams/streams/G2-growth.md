# G2 · growth: find the first people who want this

**Outcome.** The owner has an evidence-backed view of:
- which early-adopter groups to court first, and what each needs that the app doesn't do yet;
- where to find them;
- how Spoken Margins honestly compares with the apps they use now;
- a repeatable method for inbound content (pages like "best podcast apps for note-takers") that stays accurate as competitors change.

**Why.** Today the app is used by people who are testing it as a favor. The next step is people who choose it.

**Where.** Cloud. Research: use the `deep-research` skill.

## Starting points

- The owner's hypotheses:
  - **Students.** They read and listen a lot and must think critically. They'd need class Margins kept apart from personal ones.
  - **Personal-knowledge-management people** (Obsidian, Notion, note-taking methods).
  - **Creators with communities**, who could run book clubs.
- Existing research: `docs/pkm-product-plan-2026-09-12.md`, `docs/pkm-workflows.md`, `Design/SpokenMarginsOverhaul/research/`.
- Related issues: #113, #174 (invite-only beta with PKM creators), #175 (App Store checklist), #173 (legal setup before the public release).

## Milestones

**M1: Segments.**
- Done when, for at least three segments, a brief on the tracking issue gives:
  - the jobs they're trying to do;
  - the tools they use now;
  - where they gather (named communities, newsletters, forums);
  - five interview questions;
  - the product gaps that would stop them, each linked to a stream or a new issue;
  - the smallest test of real interest the owner could run in two weeks.

**M2: Honest comparison.**
- Done when a comparison of at least 10 apps people would otherwise use (listening, read-aloud, podcast and note-taking tools) is kept as a data file with sources and dates, plus a documented refresh method: which fields change, where to check, how often.
- Every claim about another product cites a source and a date.

**M3: Inbound method.**
- Done when a written method exists for:
  - choosing topics (search intent, fit with the segments);
  - page templates;
  - keeping comparison pages current from the M2 data file;
  - measuring whether it works.
- Three example outlines are included. Nothing is published without the owner.

## Must not break

Honesty: no fake reviews, no unsourced claims about competitors, no pretending to be a neutral third party.

## Out of scope

Paid acquisition. Writing and publishing the articles (a follow-up once the owner approves the method).
