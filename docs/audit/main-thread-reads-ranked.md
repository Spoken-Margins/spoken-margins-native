## What the read-cost table priced (#2513)

129 functions that were unmeasured now carry a cost from the read-cost table (120 measured, 9 partial, a lower bound because a read is not in the table). 48 non-stay functions are still unmeasured: they have no store read the table prices. Notes on how to read the new numbers:

- A function's cost is the sum of the reads the audit listed for it, each at the table's Library-scale milliseconds. A by-id read with no qualifier is priced at the table's row for it (the mapped podcast or one article), so it is a typical case, not the worst. Writes are not priced.
- The seeded tables are assumptions (see `LibraryFixtureTables.swift`): no export of the owner's job, manifest or download counts exists. Treat a cost that rests on them (prepared audio manifests above all) as an order of magnitude until a Mac-side count replaces the assumption.
- Rows already `measured` or `estimated` were left as they were.
