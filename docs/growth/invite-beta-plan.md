# Invite-only beta plan (DRAFT for Cole to approve or cut down)

> **Nobody has been contacted.** No messages, posts, TestFlight groups or links exist for this plan. Every claim about a person comes from a public page opened on 2026-09-30; re-open each source before writing to anyone, because shows go quiet and links move (#453, part of #174).

## The plan in five lines

1. Invite about 12 people, by name, through TestFlight email invitations. Start with podcasters whose shows have a public feed, because the first thing they see is a Margin on their own episode.
2. Each invitation leads with a gift: a short spoken Margin on a specific moment of their episode or post, sent as a link. Without the app the link shows the note, the moment and their episode or post; with the app it plays at that point.
3. The ask is small: try it for two weeks, and reply with what got in the way. What they get: early input on what we build next, thanks by name when the app launches, if they want it, and the first thing they ask for goes to the top of the list.
4. Three measures: finishing setup without help, still listening in week two, and Margins shared.
5. Nothing goes out until share links, the privacy policy and the legal basics are done (the checklist at the end).

## 4. Three measures

The app has no analytics or tracking code, by design (see `docs/legal/privacy-policy-draft.md`), so these are read from TestFlight's own tester data and from what testers tell Cole. Small numbers: read them person by person, not as percentages.

| Measure | What counts | How it's read | Good looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Setup without help** | The tester installs, gets through the tour, and plays one item without writing to Cole for help | App Store Connect's TestFlight tester list shows each tester's status and sessions ([tester metrics](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/view-and-manage-tester-information/)). Cole counts anyone who has sessions and never asked for help. A day-3 check-in asks one question: "Did anything stop you getting started?" | 8 of 12 get started unaided |
| **Week-two use** | The tester opens the app in days 8 to 14 after installing | TestFlight's per-tester sessions count, which can lag up to 24 hours ([same page](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/view-and-manage-tester-information/)). Cole notes each tester's count at day 7 and day 14; a higher count at day 14 means they came back | 5 of 12 come back in week two |
| **Margins shared** | The tester sends at least one Margin to someone else | Before share links exist: ask at day 14. After: the server's hourly counts of new links are anonymous and can't say who shared, so the day-14 question stays the source per tester, and the server total is a cross-check | 3 of 12 share one |

**The day-14 message** asks three things: what got in the way, what they'd want next, and whether they shared a Margin. TestFlight's own feedback (screenshots sent from the beta) also counts.

## Defaults taken

- **Named invitations over a public link.** Options: named email invitations, or a capped public link. Pick: named, because the plan is 12 hand-picked people, and a link can be forwarded. #174 still lists this as open for Cole.
- **Twelve in the first wave, big names later.** Options: all 16 at once, or a smaller first wave. Pick: 12, so Cole can record a real Margin for each one and answer every reply.
- **Option B if share links are late.** Options: wait for share links, or start with screen recordings. Pick: recordings, so the beta isn't blocked on #486 and #487; the link version replaces them once it ships.
- **Say AI tools help build it.** Options: "solo developer", or "one person, with AI coding tools". Pick: the second, because most of the code is written with AI agents and the G2 honesty rules rule out anything a reader could call misleading. Cole can reword it.
