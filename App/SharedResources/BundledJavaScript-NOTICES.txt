Spoken Margins bundled JavaScript notices

These open-source packages are compiled into the scripts Spoken Margins
uses to read web pages. Each package's license follows.

browserTextCapture.js: @xmldom/xmldom, defuddle, mathml-to-latex, temml, turndown
SourceLinkPreprocessor.js: @xmldom/xmldom, defuddle, mathml-to-latex, temml, turndown
popup.js: no third-party packages
