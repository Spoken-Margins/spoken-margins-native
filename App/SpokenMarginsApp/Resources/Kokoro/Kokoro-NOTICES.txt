Kokoro voice runtime: sources and licenses

Spoken Margins runs the downloadable Kokoro voice with the KokoroPipeline Swift library from kokoro-coreml by Matt Mireles (https://github.com/mattmireles/kokoro-coreml, commit e8bf3d4e80d7dd30ed58e343c1527fad09cc9d1f), licensed under the Apache License 2.0. It runs the voice's Core ML stages on this iPhone's Neural Engine and CPU.

Its text front end is the bundled fallback voice's (see "Fallback voice" in this list), respelled into Kokoro's phoneme set with the espeak-to-misaki table from misaki by hexgrad (https://github.com/hexgrad/misaki), Apache-2.0. No GPL code or data is included; espeak-ng is not used.

The Kokoro-82M model, its voices and their licenses are downloaded with the voice from Settings › Voices, and listed there.
