Notice of modification (BigScience Open RAIL-M, paragraph 4(c))

The files duration_predictor.onnx, text_encoder.onnx, vector_estimator.onnx
and vocoder.onnx in this folder are modified derivatives of Supertone's
Supertonic 3 model (https://huggingface.co/Supertone/supertonic-3, revision
3cadd1ee6394adea1bd021217a0e650ede09a323).

They were changed as follows:
1. The k2-fsa sherpa-onnx project quantized Supertone's public ONNX graphs to
   INT8 (archive sherpa-onnx-supertonic-3-tts-int8-2026-05-11.tar.bz2).
2. Spoken Margins renamed the quantized graph files to match the official
   Supertonic Swift runtime and packaged them with the official configuration,
   Unicode index and preset voice styles, which are unchanged.

The model remains licensed under BigScience Open RAIL-M; see
../licenses/SUPERTONIC_MODEL_OPENRAIL-M.txt and ../licenses/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt.
