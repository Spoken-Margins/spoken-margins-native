Core ML conversion
The files in coreml/, voices/ and runtime/ are the Core ML conversion of
Kokoro-82M published by Matt Mireles as mattmireles/kokoro-coreml
(https://huggingface.co/mattmireles/kokoro-coreml, revision
0c1d78b47d19f39be6bc0cf0d07316822ae879d4), licensed under the Apache
License 2.0. Spoken Margins downloads them unchanged; the compiled/ folder
is made on this device from them by Core ML.

Pronunciation dictionaries
The files in lexicon/ are misaki's American English dictionaries, us_gold.json and us_silver.json, by hexgrad
(https://github.com/hexgrad/misaki, commit e820629b96334db28227df37f280e4836d46fadb), licensed under the Apache
License 2.0 (the complete license is included as APACHE-2.0.txt). Spoken Margins downloads them unchanged and
makes a sorted copy on this device (lexicon/misaki-us.tsv) for looking words up. Kokoro was trained on text
phonemized with these dictionaries, so the app reads text with them first.
