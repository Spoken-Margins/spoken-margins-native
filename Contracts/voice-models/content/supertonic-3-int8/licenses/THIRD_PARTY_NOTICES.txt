INT8 model conversion
The bundled ONNX graphs are modified, quantized derivatives of Supertonic 3.
They were produced by the k2-fsa sherpa-onnx project from Supertone's public
ONNX assets. Spoken Margins renamed the graph files for the official Swift
runtime contract and packaged them with the official configuration, Unicode
index, and preset voice-style files. The sherpa-onnx Apache 2.0 license is
included as SHERPA_ONNX_APACHE-2.0.txt.
