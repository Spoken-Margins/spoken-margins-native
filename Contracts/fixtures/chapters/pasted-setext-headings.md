Garden diary
============

Spring
------

The first shoots came up in the second week of March. I cleared the beds and turned in the compost.

Summer
------

The beans did well. The courgettes did too well and we gave them away to anyone who stood still.

Autumn
------

We saved seed from the beans and lifted the potatoes before the first frost.

Winter
------

Planning, mostly. A catalogue arrived and I made a list that is already too long.
