# Why our deploys got boring

We used to dread Friday afternoons. This is how we made releases something nobody noticed.

## The old pipeline

Every release meant a freeze, a checklist of forty items, and one person holding the pager for the weekend.

**Key point:**

The checklist was longer than the code change it protected.

## Small changes, shipped often

We cut changes down until each one could be explained in a sentence.

**What we did**

Merge to main several times a day behind a flag.

**What we didn't do**

Add another review stage.

## Rollbacks as a normal tool

A rollback is a button, not an incident. We practise it monthly.

**Takeaway:**

If you are afraid of rolling back, you are not ready to ship.

## What we would change

We would have invested in observability earlier. The first month was mostly guessing.
